      How to play

      1. Each player gets 7 cards. The rest form the pond.
      2. On your turn, pick a rank you hold and ask your opponent for it.
      3. If they have any, they give you all of them and you go again.
      4. If not — “Go fish!” Draw a card from the pond; your turn ends.
      5. Collect all four cards of a rank to make a book. Making a book lets you play again.
      6. If your hand is empty, you automatically draw from the pond.
      7. When all 13 books are made, whoever has the most books wins.

      House rule “Fish your wish”: if you draw the rank you asked for, show it and go again.
